# 💼 JobFind – Job Search Portal

JobFind is a web-based job search platform designed to make job discovery easier. It allows users to explore job opportunities by searching for job titles, keywords, company names, and locations through a simple and user-friendly interface.

## 🚀 Live Demo

[Click here to explore JobFind](https://studio-hsikxrewl-jagdaleradhika6-glitchs-projects.vercel.app)

## ✨ Features

- **Job Search:** Search for jobs using job titles, keywords, and company names.
- **Location-Based Search:** Find opportunities based on city, state, or remote location.
- **Job Listings:** Browse available job opportunities in an organized layout.
- **Job Details:** View additional information about job opportunities.
- **Job Categories:** Explore different employment types, including internships and contract positions.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Clean and simple design for a better job-searching experience.
- **Admin Navigation:** Dedicated admin section for administrative functionality.
